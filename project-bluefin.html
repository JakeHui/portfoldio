The challenge

Develop an underwater surveying vehicle for fossil exploration at Turlock Lake. Visibility, obstacle navigation, and sediment were central challenges during field testing.

My role

I led a six-person team, coordinated work with paleontology researchers, and organized a school trip to test the vehicle at the lake. I secured approximately $6,000 in research grants to support development.

Development & field testing

The project integrated feedback from institutions including the LA Natural History Museum and the Western Science Center. Testing in the lake connected the engineering work to the conditions the vehicle would encounter in use.

The next iteration

In a subsequent two-person effort, we developed a custom autonomous six-degree-of-freedom vehicle with a three-degree-of-freedom arm as a low-cost, open-source platform.
