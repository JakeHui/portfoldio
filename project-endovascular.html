Research overview

At the Biomechatronics & Intelligent Robotics Lab, I contributed to the design and refinement of a surgical robot for endovascular procedures. My work focused on mechanical prototypes for precise catheter and guidewire manipulation.

My contribution

As an undergraduate student researcher, I created 3D CAD models and mechanical prototypes, contributing to the robot’s design and refinement from June to September 2026.

Mechanical assemblies

The CAD images document roller assemblies, geared mechanisms, and their integration into a module housing. Front, side, and component views show how the rollers, shafts, gears, and supporting parts fit together.

Physical prototypes

The assembled modules bring the mechanical design into physical form. The prototype photograph shows three modules alongside one another, while the CAD views provide a closer look at the internal mechanisms and housings.
