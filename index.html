Research

June 2026–Present · NYU Prema Lab

Scafish — modular robotic fish

A modular robotic fish for studying bio-inspired underwater locomotion at NYU Prema Lab. My work focuses on CAD design, fabrication, and Python control of Dynamixel servos.

I develop control routines and visualize tail position and motor velocity to support motion testing, from bench tests to pool trials.

Project details

Ongoing

2023–Present · Team lead · Six-person research team

BlueFin

I led a six-person team developing an underwater surveying vehicle for fossil exploration at Turlock Lake. Field testing addressed visibility, obstacle navigation, and sediment challenges, with feedback from the LA Natural History Museum and the Western Science Center. I secured approximately $6,000 in research grants to support development.

In a subsequent two-person effort, we developed a custom autonomous six-degree-of-freedom vehicle with a three-degree-of-freedom arm as a low-cost, open-source platform.

Project details

January 2025–Present · Co-designer · Team NEET

Combat robotics — Team NEET

As part of a two-person team, I co-designed and built Rem & Ram, a shuffler-based beater bar multibot for NHRL competition. Its first iteration competed in February 2026.

Across Team NEET, we developed more than 13 different robots and competed in four events.

2025

2024–2025 · Team lead · FTC 359

Into the Deep

I led Team 359 in developing a fully custom robot with a three-degree-of-freedom extending arm, servo-driven linkages, and a belt-driven drivetrain. The mechanical systems were integrated with multithreaded control and odometry-based autonomous pathing.
