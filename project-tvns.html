The brief

Improve the accessibility, usability, and cost efficiency of a transcutaneous vagus nerve stimulation (tVNS) device.

My contribution

Design considerations

I analyzed high costs, usability challenges, and aesthetic concerns in existing models. The team explored design approaches and technical limitations with the aim of improving accessibility and user experience.
