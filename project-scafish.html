Research overview

Scafish is a modular robotic fish developed through my undergraduate research at NYU Prema Lab. The project investigates bio-inspired underwater locomotion through mechanical prototyping and controlled tail motion.

Mechanical design & fabrication

I lead CAD design and fabrication of the modular fish. The prototype combines a segmented tail structure, a servo-driven mechanism, and an assembled body for motion testing. The photos document the exposed structure and the assembled prototype.

Control & testing

I develop Python control routines for Dynamixel servos and visualize tail position and motor velocity to support motion testing. The videos show bench testing of the mechanism and pool testing of the assembled fish.

Ongoing development

My current work connects mechanical iteration with motion testing: refining the prototype, adjusting control routines, and examining the resulting tail motion.
