Team & leadership

I joined FTC Team 359 WEBB.EXE in 2021, led the team from 2022–2025, and continue as an alumni advisor on mechanical design and team management. The team qualified for the FIRST World Championships in the 2025–2026 season.

Across my work with the team: six custom FTC robots, three SoCal championship qualifications, and more than ten team awards.

2025–2026 · DECODE

I support the team as an alumni mentor and help with CAD on DECODE v1 and v2. The team qualified for the FIRST World Championships in the 2025–2026 season.

2024–2025 · Into the Deep

I led FTC Team 359 WEBB.EXE during the 2024–2025 Into the Deep season. Our work combined custom mechanical design with autonomous control.

The robot used a three-degree-of-freedom extending arm, servo-driven linkage systems, and a belt-driven drivetrain.

Multithreaded control coordinated the mechanical systems. Odometry-based autonomous pathing supported autonomous navigation.

2023–2024 · Centerstage

I led FTC Team 359 WEBB.EXE in the 2023–2024 Centerstage season, developing a fully custom competition robot.

The robot featured a differential four-degree-of-freedom end effector, a six-stage linear slide extension, and a belt-driven chassis.

Multithreaded control coordinated the mechanisms, with sensor-based automation and odometry-driven autonomous navigation.

2022–2023 · POWERPLAY

I led the team during the POWERPLAY season. The robot is included in the design history above.
