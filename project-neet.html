The concept

Rem & Ram is a shuffler-based beater bar multibot developed for NHRL competition.

My role

I co-designed and built the robots as part of Team NEET, a two-person team.

Competition & iteration

The first iteration of Rem & Ram competed in February 2026. Across Team NEET, we developed more than 13 different robots and competed in four events.

Mechanical design & fabrication

We developed more than 13 robot designs in Fusion 360 and produced four physical competition builds, including vertical spinners and undercutters.

Drivetrain development included wheeled, shuffler, and gyroscopic-precession-based movement. Both team members contributed to mechanical design, fabrication, and testing.
